## The challenge

Greenfield Academy is a concept school we use to show how we approach a nursery-to-secondary school website. It reflects the brief we hear most often from Nigerian proprietors: *"Our school is excellent, but our website doesn't show it."*

The typical starting point looks like this:

- A dated website (or a Facebook page) that loads slowly on mobile data
- Admission forms sold at the gate and filled by hand
- Results shared as PDFs in WhatsApp groups
- Fee payments confirmed by screenshots of bank alerts

Parents in other cities, and returning diaspora families, judge the school from their phones long before they visit. The website has to earn that first visit.

## What we built

A calm, editorial school website with an ivy-green identity and a crest-led design, built mobile-first and tested on budget Android phones over 3G.

Behind the front page sit the modules that do the real work:

- **Online admissions** with document uploads, form-fee payment and entrance-exam scheduling
- **A result checker** that accepts scratch-card PINs and generates printable report cards
- **Fee payments** through Paystack, with automatic receipts and a bursar dashboard
- **News, events and a gallery** that staff update themselves from a simple dashboard

Everything shares one database with our school management system, so an approved application becomes a student record without anyone retyping it.

## Results

This is a concept project, so there are no client results to report yet. When a real school launches a site like this, we'll measure what matters to proprietors: online applications, time saved in the admissions office, and fees collected without chasing.

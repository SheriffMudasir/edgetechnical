## The school

Al-Mu&rsquo;minun International Academy in Kwamba, Suleja (Niger State) runs Nursery, Primary, Junior and Senior Secondary sections. It combines the Nigerian national curriculum with an Islamic curriculum and an intensive Tahfeez al-Qur&rsquo;an programme, with day and boarding students.

## The challenge

A school like this has a lot to explain to parents: three academic sections, a dual curriculum, Tahfeez, boarding, and admissions for a new session. And behind the scenes, staff need one place to manage applicants, students, results and attendance.

The school needed:

- A website that presents its identity clearly, in English and Arabic
- Admissions that parents can start online, from anywhere
- A way for parents to follow an application and, once enrolled, see their child&rsquo;s progress
- One system where staff manage applicants, students, results and attendance

## What we built

**A public school website.** Home, About, Programs, Admissions, News & Events and Contact pages, with bilingual English and Arabic typography, section-by-section programme pages from Nursery to SSS 3, and a visiting page with map directions, Google Plus Code and postal details for the Kwamba campus.

**Online admissions.** A clear four-step roadmap and an online application form. Each submission generates a unique reference number (for example APP/2026/0001).

**Application tracking.** Parents check their application status with their reference number and the phone number they registered.

**A school management system** connected to the website:

- A **staff and admin portal** for teachers and administrators
- Approved applicants converted into students, each with a permanent admission number and portal access
- A **parent and guardian portal** with secure one-time sign-in by email, giving access to published term report cards, grades and attendance

One system powers both the public website and the school&rsquo;s day-to-day administration.

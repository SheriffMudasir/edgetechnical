## The challenge

Millions of Nigerian students prepare for exams on phones with patchy, expensive data. Most "AI tutors" assume a fast, constant connection, and most of them simply hand students the answer, which helps with homework but not with exam day.

We wanted to build something different: a tutor that works in a village with one bar of signal, and one that makes students think.

## What we built

**StudyWise AI** is our own product: an offline-first exam-prep app for Nigerian students.

- **On-device AI.** The tutor runs on the phone itself, so lessons, practice and tutoring keep working with no connection at all. Progress syncs quietly when the network returns.
- **Socratic, multi-persona tutoring.** Instead of giving answers, StudyWise asks the next good question. Students can switch between tutor personalities: a patient explainer, a quick-fire drill coach, or a strict examiner.
- **Built for Nigerian exams.** Practice is shaped around the papers Nigerian students actually sit, with explanations in clear, everyday English.
- **AI Points via Paystack.** No forced subscriptions. Students or parents top up AI Points when they need more tutoring.

## What it taught us

Building StudyWise taught us how students really learn, and how unforgiving real-world networks and devices are. That experience goes straight into the school websites, portals and offline apps we build for schools.

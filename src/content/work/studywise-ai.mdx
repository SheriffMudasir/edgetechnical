## The challenge

Millions of Nigerian students prepare for WAEC, NECO and JAMB on phones with patchy, expensive data. Most study tools either assume a fast connection or simply hand students the answer, which helps with homework but not with exam day.

We wanted to build something different: exam prep that is genuinely engaging, that makes students think, and that keeps working in the conditions students actually study in.

## What we built

**StudyWise AI** is our own product, live at [studywiseai.org](https://www.studywiseai.org) and free to start.

- **Built for WAEC, NECO and JAMB.** Tailored practice modes for JAMB (UTME), WAEC (SSCE) and NECO (SSCE), with an extensive bank of past questions and detailed solutions.
- **AI-powered insights.** Smart feedback on practice questions shows students their weak areas and suggests what to work on next.
- **A crew of tutors.** Six characters (Ada, Aisha, Baba, Ijeoma, Tunde and Zino) guide and motivate students, each with their own personality, from calm scholar to energetic gamer.
- **Teaches rather than tells.** The tutoring is Socratic: it asks the next good question instead of handing over the answer.
- **Gamified.** Points, badges and weekly leaderboards turn revision into a challenge, and progress tracking shows how ready a student is for the real exam.
- **Designed for real conditions.** Offline-first, with on-device AI for students in low-connectivity areas, and AI Points paid via Paystack instead of forced subscriptions.

## What it taught us

Building StudyWise taught us how students really learn, and how unforgiving real-world networks and devices are. That experience goes straight into the school websites, portals and apps we build for schools.

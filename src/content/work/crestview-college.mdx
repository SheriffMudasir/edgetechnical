## The challenge

Crestview College is a concept secondary school we use to show our school management system. Its problem is one we hear in almost every staff room: result season.

- Teachers enter scores in separate Excel files that someone has to merge
- Form masters calculate totals and positions by hand, late into the night
- Report cards are typed one by one, and errors are found after printing
- Parents call the school to ask about balances and attendance

## What we built

A school management system designed around how Nigerian secondary schools actually run.

- **Results entry by teachers.** Subject teachers enter CA 1, CA 2 and exam scores for their own classes only. Totals, grades and positions calculate themselves.
- **Report-card PDFs.** Beautifully formatted report cards with the school crest, WAEC/NECO-style grading, remarks and attendance, generated for a whole class in seconds.
- **Attendance.** Registers taken on a phone in under a minute, with optional absence alerts to parents.
- **Parent logins by phone number.** Parents see results, attendance and fee balances without remembering a username.
- **An offline-first teacher app.** Registers and scores save on the phone and sync when the network returns.

It connects directly to the school's website, so results flow to the result checker and payments update balances automatically.

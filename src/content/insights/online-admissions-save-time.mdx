Every admissions season, the same scene plays out at school gates across Nigeria. Parents queue to buy forms. They take them home, fill them by hand, and bring them back with photocopies of birth certificates. Someone in the office types the details into a spreadsheet. Someone else calls each family to schedule an entrance exam.

It works, after a fashion. But it's slow, it loses applicants, and it eats weeks of your staff's time. Here's what changes when admissions move online.

## Parents apply when it suits them

Most parents research schools in the evening, after work, on their phones. With online admissions, they can apply right then, while they're still interested, instead of waiting for a weekday when they can get to your gate.

That matters most for the families you'd otherwise miss: parents relocating from another city, families abroad planning a return, and busy professionals who can't take time off to buy a form.

## No more lost forms or illegible handwriting

An online form asks for exactly what you need, in the format you need it. Required fields can't be skipped. Phone numbers and email addresses are checked as they're typed. Birth certificates, passport photos and previous results are uploaded directly.

Your admissions team no longer spends hours deciphering handwriting or chasing missing documents.

## Form fees are paid, not promised

With Paystack or Flutterwave built in, parents pay the application fee online by card, transfer or USSD as part of applying. The payment is linked to the application automatically, so there's no reconciling bank alerts against names.

## Everything lands in one dashboard

Instead of a stack of paper, your team sees every application in one place. You can filter by class, see which documents are missing, and move applicants through stages: received, assessment booked, offered, accepted.

When several staff members are involved, everyone sees the same, up-to-date picture.

## Entrance exams schedule themselves

Parents can choose an assessment date when they apply, or you can invite applicants to a session with one click. Confirmations and reminders go out by SMS and email, so fewer families forget to turn up.

## Offers go out faster

When you've made a decision, offer letters can be sent in minutes rather than days. A fast, professional response is part of the impression your school makes, and families comparing several schools often accept the first good offer they receive.

## Applicants become students without retyping

If your website is connected to your school management system, an accepted applicant becomes a student record automatically. Their details, documents and guardian information flow straight through. Nobody types them in again.

## What it costs you to stay on paper

It's easy to underestimate the cost of the paper process, because it's spread across many people and many small tasks. Count the hours your staff spend selling forms, typing details, calling parents and filing documents during one admissions season, and the case usually makes itself.

And the applicants you never hear from, because buying a form was too much trouble, don't show up in any spreadsheet at all.
